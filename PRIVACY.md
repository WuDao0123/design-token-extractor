# Privacy policy — Design Token Extractor

Last updated: 2026-10-29

Design Token Extractor does not collect, store, sell or transmit any personal or browsing data.
When you click the extension, it reads the computed visual styles (colors, fonts, sizes, spacing) of the current tab locally in your browser and shows them in the popup. Exports (SKILL.md / JSON) are created locally and saved only where you choose.
The extension makes no network requests, loads no remote code, and contains no analytics or tracking.
